Green Energy
Transformation Cycle

Three integrated strategic pillars: securing 100% certified renewable energy, generating on-site solar power, and cutting consumption through production equipment electrification.

All electricity consumed across Pratama Group is certified by 100% Renewable Energy Certificates (REC) from PT PLN (Persero) Indonesia. Every kWh used is verified from renewable generation — an audited, official sustainability credential.
